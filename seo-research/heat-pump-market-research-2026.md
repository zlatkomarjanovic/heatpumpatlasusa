## Executive Summary

**Is this still worth building?** Yes, but not as a 10,000-page city atlas. The national commercial SERPs are weaker than the previous 69.4 score implied. The geo long-tail is much weaker than that score assumed.

**Did deeper research strengthen or weaken the thesis?** It split the thesis.

What got stronger:

- `heat pump installation cost` is 22,200 monthly US searches, KD 9, CPC $9.97. Reddit ranks #1. A UK YouTube video and a Canadian Carrier page rank in the US SERP.
- Mini-split cost terms are large and similarly messy.
- Calculator SERPs have no national winner. Rural co-ops, ENERGY STAR, EPRI, and a pool-heater calculator rank.
- HVAC lead prices in 2026 support a real business if you can sell exclusive or high-intent quotes: shared form leads $25-$90, exclusive form leads $100-$264, Google Ads HVAC often $190-$335 CPL, heating install campaigns around $354 CPL.

What got weaker, and this matters more than the headline volumes:

- Google Ads returned **zero volume for 218 of 270 state-pattern keywords**. `heat pump cost texas` and `heat pump cost chicago` are not real markets.
- Of 78 priority city keywords, **only 8 had any volume**. Best city term found: `heat pump cost seattle` at 50/month. Denver 20. Boston, New York, Dallas, Miami, Los Angeles: no measurable exact-match volume.
- Federal 25C tax credit expired 31 December 2025. `heat pump tax credit` is 1,900 searches and falling (yearly -47% on the related rebate cluster). IRS and ENERGY STAR still rank as if the credit is live.
- Almost every national cost/comparison query has an AI Overview.
- State rebate programs pause, reserve, and change amounts. California single-family HEEHRA was fully reserved by 24 February 2026.
- Several 2026 rebate-tracking sites already exist (Heat Pump Network, RebatePeek, ElectrifyAtlas) and have almost no traffic. That is a warning: rebate encyclopedias are easy to publish and hard to rank or keep accurate.

**Best target keyword clusters**

1. National installed-cost + calculator (`heat pump installation cost`, `heat pump cost`, `how much does a heat pump cost`)
2. Mini-split installed cost by zones
3. Heat pump vs furnace / vs oil, with local energy prices
4. Named rebate programs and utilities (Mass Save, Efficiency Maine, Xcel, Eversource, PG&E), not `heat pump rebates [random state]`
5. Adjacent jobs that share the quote form: furnace replacement, panel upgrade, HPWH, EV charger

**Best states:** Maine, Massachusetts, Vermont, Colorado, California (rebates, not cheap power), Oregon, Washington, New Hampshire, New York, Connecticut, Rhode Island.

**Best cities:** Almost none on search volume. If you launch cities, do it for installer coverage, not SEO: Boston, Denver, Seattle, Portland (OR), Portland (ME), Minneapolis, Hartford, Albany, Buffalo, Burlington.

**Best calculator:** A ZIP-based **total cost of switching** tool. Installed cost range + local operating cost vs current fuel + active program rebates in launch markets + payback + quote CTA. Not a national average widget. Not a 50-state rebate scraper.

**Biggest risks**

1. City/state pSEO does not have demand.
2. AI Overviews already answer the generic question.
3. Rebate data will rot faster than you can write pages.
4. High electricity prices make gas-to-heat-pump savings claims easy to get wrong.
5. You do not have an installer network. EnergySage and Angi do.
6. Volume on the biggest cost terms is seasonally spiky and down year over year on several heads.

**Recommended decision:** **BUILD WITH CONDITIONS**

Build a thin, high-quality site: national hubs, one serious calculator, ~20 program/utility pages, ~10 launch-market pages, and supporting articles. Do not build a programmatic city grid.

## Best Rebate Opportunities

Rebate search is the only geo cluster that clearly exists. It is also the highest-maintenance and the most .gov-dominated.

**Build**

- Mass Save (1,900, KD 2, CPC $13.85). You will not outrank MassSave.com. You can outrank contractor blogs and Reddit with a 2026 stacking explainer plus calculator.
- Efficiency Maine / Maine rebates (720-880). Same pattern.
- Colorado / Xcel ($33.73 CPC on the state term).
- California TECH / HEEHRA status (reserved, so the useful page is “what is still open,” not “claim $8,000”).
- Eversource, National Grid, PG&E, SCE, ComEd, PSEG, Duke, BGE, NYSERDA (110-210 each).

**Do not build first**

- `heat pump rebates [state]` for the 30+ states with 0-10 volume.
- A live scraper of every utility rebate in America.
- A 25C tax-credit funnel. The credit is dead for 2026 installs. Official pages have not caught up. Ranking for it with outdated copy would be malpractice.

HEAR/HOMES still exist as state programs, with uneven launch and fast sellouts. That is a product feature (status + eligibility), not a 50-page doorway set.

## Heat Pump vs Furnace Opportunity

`heat pump vs furnace` is 6,600, KD 13, CPC $6.50. `heat pump vs gas furnace` is 1,600, KD 6.

SERP is medium: Carrier, Trane, Lennox, Goodman, Reddit, YouTube, and the American Gas Association page that says gas is cheaper in 36 states.

That AGA page is the opening. A ZIP tool that uses EIA electricity and gas prices, climate, and furnace AFUE can beat every OEM brochure. It will also show that **in many gas states the heat pump loses on operating cost**. Publish that. A calculator that always “saves $1,200/year” will get destroyed in comments and by AI citation.

`heat pump vs oil` is the better conversion story and a weaker SERP (Reddit, Facebook, EnergySage, oil dealers, local MA contractors). Exact national volume is low; Trends interest is concentrated in Maine, Vermont, Massachusetts, New Hampshire.

State-level `vs furnace` volume is essentially zero except Colorado (30). So the comparison page should be national, with state modules generated from real rates, not 50 thin URLs.

## Calculator Strategy

Current calculators:

| Tool | Inputs | Weakness |
|---|---|---|
| Rewiring America Electrification Planner | ZIP, income, home facts | Incentive-first, 25C residue, no installer close |
| ENERGY STAR savings calculator | Equipment type, crude home | Ugly, not local, not rebate-aware |
| EPRI residential heating calculator | Technical lifecycle | Engineer UX, not a homeowner funnel |
| Mass Save / Efficiency Maine | State rates | Geography-locked |
| CEMC, RVEC, Prairie Energy | Co-op territory | Rank nationally by accident |
| Homewyse | Quantity, ZIP-ish | Generic trade pricing |
| Pentair | Pool | Wrong intent, still ranks |
| EPA/NREL ASHP estimator (research) | Vintage, sqft, climate, fuel | Not a consumer brand yet |

**Core product (confirmed, not replaced):**

A **Switching Cost Calculator**.

Inputs:

- ZIP (required)
- Heated sqft
- Current heating fuel (gas, oil, propane, electric resistance, existing heat pump)
- Current annual heating spend or winter bills (optional, calibrates the model)
- Ducted vs ductless vs mixed
- Number of zones if ductless
- Home age / insulation quality (simple 3-level)
- Electrical panel: 100A / 200A / unknown
- Household income band only if a launch rebate is income-gated

Outputs:

- Installed cost range (equipment + labor + likely electrical)
- Annual operating cost vs current system
- 10-year undiscounted cost (and a simple payback)
- Rebates that are *open in this ZIP among programs you actually maintain*
- “Insufficient data / program paused” rather than a fake $8,000
- Optional CO2
- Quote CTA: send this scenario to 1-3 installers

ZIP is required. Without ZIP the tool is another national average, and those already lose to Reddit.

**Do not make the core tool a rebate lookup.** Rebate lookup is a feature. The job to be done is “what will this cost me, and is it stupid.”

Secondary tools, later:

- Mini-split room/zone sizer (BTU), after the money tool works
- HPWH payback
- Panel-upgrade add-on

## Monetization

Evidence, 2026:

- Google Ads HVAC CPL commonly $190-$335 (99 Calls, H1 2026). Heating install campaigns ~$354 CPL (SearchLight).
- Shared marketplace form leads $25-$90, sold to 4-5 contractors, close rate often 5-12%.
- Exclusive form leads $100-$264, close 20-35% when follow-up is fast.
- Exclusive live calls $65-$325 (Service Direct ranges).
- Heat pump jobs are $8,000-$25,000+ installed before incentives. Contractor gross profit can absorb a $150-$250 exclusive lead if it is exclusive and qualified.
- EnergySage charges installers for marketplace access (fee card not public). Homeowners pick the company. Better lead quality story than Angi.
- Angi/Thumbtack are the default and the ones contractors complain about.

**Lead value estimates (what you can charge a contractor, not what Google charges them)**

| Case | Price to contractor | Assumption |
|---|---:|---|
| Conservative | $40 | Shared, 3-way, soft intent |
| Realistic | $120 | Exclusive form, ZIP + fuel + sqft attached |
| Aggressive | $220 | Exclusive, phone-verified, launch metro only |

Not every calculator completion is a lead. Use:

- 8-15% of calculator completions request a quote
- 50-70% of quote requests are contactable
- 20-30% exclusive close if the installer is competent

**Monthly revenue scenarios (exclusive $120 blended, 25% of form fills actually exclusive and in-network)**

These are not “100 form fills = $12,000.”

|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|

        \-> you will share this SERP with maps and program sites

Highest conversion: calculator result page and geo/program pages where the next step is a quote.

## Risks

1. **Geo demand was overstated.** City pSEO does not survive Ads volume checks.
2. **25C is gone.** A lot of 2023-2025 content, including official pages, is wrong.
3. **Operating-cost honesty.** At 29-35 c/kWh, heat pumps lose to gas in many homes. Oil/propane is the conversion that sells itself.
4. **Rebate rot.** Programs pause in weeks. California already did.
5. **AI Overviews** take the simple answer. Your page must be the tool.
6. **Installer supply.** Without exclusive partners, you become a worse Angi.
7. **Seasonality.** March volumes can be 2-3x July. Cash flow will spike.
8. **KD looks easier than the SERP is.** KD 9 with Reddit #1 is real, but Bryant/Rewiring America/This Old House are not amateur blogs.
9. **New rebate sites already failed to gain traffic.** Directory strategy is crowded and unpaid.
10. **Legal/accuracy.** Do not republish AHRI catalogs or DSIRE wholesale. Do not imply a reserved rebate is available.
