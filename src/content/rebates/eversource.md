The **Eversource heat pump rebate** depends on your service address. Start with **Mass Save in Massachusetts**, **Energize CT in Connecticut**, or **NHSaves in New Hampshire**. Massachusetts Eversource electric customers can also check eligibility for a lower winter delivery rate.

## Find your program by state

| Service address | Eligibility to check | Where to apply |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Massachusetts | Mass Save sponsor, qualifying equipment and installer. Whole-home projects have a verification form. | [Mass Save rebate requirements and forms](https://www.masssave.com/residential/rebates-offers-services/heating-and-cooling/heat-pumps/air-source-heat-pumps) |
| Connecticut | Check the eligible replacement fuel, equipment, installer and any steps required before work begins. | [Energize CT Residential Energy Optimization](https://www.energizect.com/rebates-incentives/heating-cooling/heat-pumps/residential-energy-optimization) |
| New Hampshire | Use the Eversource electric customer section. Check pre-verification requirements if replacing electric resistance heat. | [NHSaves heat pump rebate and application](https://nhsaves.com/residential/electric-heating-cooling-equipment/) |

For 2026 air-source systems, Mass Save lists **$2,650 per ton up to $8,500** for standard whole-home projects, **$1,125 per ton up to $8,500** for partial-home projects, and **$250 per ton up to $2,500** for basic projects. Income-qualified offers have separate terms. Get the applicable amount and application steps in writing before you pay a deposit.

## Massachusetts winter heat pump rate

For eligible Massachusetts residential electric accounts, Eversource reduces the distribution and transmission rate from **November 1 through April 30**. Eversource says it automatically enrolls customers who received a Mass Save rebate through their Eversource electric account for a heat pump installed after January 1, 2019. Otherwise, use the [Eversource heat pump rate application](https://www.eversource.com/residential/account-billing/manage-bill/about-your-bill/rates-tariffs/heat-pump-rate) to check qualifications and the documents you need.

Run the [heat pump cost calculator](/calculator/) with your ZIP code and current heating fuel for a baseline, then compare its operating-cost estimate against your own electric bill and the applicable winter tariff.

If you need the installation rebate for a Massachusetts address, [check your Mass Save tier and application steps](/rebates/mass-save/). If you are comparing heating bills, [estimate your annual running costs](/calculator/) before you count the winter rate.
